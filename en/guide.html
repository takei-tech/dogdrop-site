FIRST PLAY GUIDE

How to play

Updated September 26, 2026

Choose a game, prepare your space, and follow the on-screen instructions. Start with Level 1.

English in-app support is in preparation. These English pages are available ahead of the update. English screen labels and language options describe the upcoming update.

Choose your game

Tap Choose a game on the title screen, select a game, then tap its Play button. Follow its guide to the settings.

  • DogDrop: Levels 1–3, one minute per game.
  • DogTrail: Levels 1–3, six course patterns each, about one minute per course.
  • Cogni Drop: choose rock-paper-scissors or math, a rule and Level 1–3. One minute per game.
  • Setup and practice take additional time. Allow camera access when asked. Usage-data sharing is optional; you can play without it.

For stopping sharing or requesting deletion, see the analytics support FAQ. Privacy settings controls that preserve local records are being prepared for a future update.

Set up your device and space

Place the device securely at about waist height, with the screen facing you. Start about 1.5 m away and adjust the distance until your head and feet fit in the front camera view.

  • Leave clear space for one step to either side and behind you. Remove objects and cables, and check that the floor is not slippery.
  • Use a bright room and avoid strong backlighting. Keep both knees and feet visible.
  • Only the player should be in the camera view. Face forward and stand still for a few seconds.
  • For DogDrop and Cogni Drop, let your arms rest naturally during the posture check. For DogTrail calibration, cross your arms over your chest.

The camera preview appears only during the posture check and squat calibration, with a frame and detected joint points or lines. Practice and gameplay do not show the camera image.

DogDrop: step and squat to match dogs

Guide the falling dog to the same breed below to score points.

  1. Step left or right to move the dog one lane in that direction.
  2. When prompted with Foot back, bring the foot you moved back to the center. The dog stays in its lane. Steady yourself before the next step.
  3. Squat to make the dog drop faster, then stand up before the next squat. Move within a comfortable range.

Keep facing the camera and keep your knees and feet visible. Try the motion practice before playing.

DogTrail: follow the path with squats

The path scrolls from right to left. Squat to move the dog down and stand to move it up. Look ahead and keep the circular marker on the dog’s body inside the path.

Calibrate your comfortable range first

  1. Cross your arms over your chest. Stand naturally and hold still until the instruction advances.
  2. Slowly make a shallow squat. Hold a comfortable height when asked. You do not need to match the example’s depth.
  3. Slowly stand up and wait for the ready message.

This is not a contest to squat deeply. Play within the range you calibrated. If it feels wrong after moving the device, return to course selection and calibrate again.

  • Each course has six down-and-up movements, with standing rests between them.
  • Level 1 uses a steady pace. Level 2 varies the pace. Level 3 uses different lowering and rising times. Higher levels also narrow the path.
  • One Level 3 movement uses the bottom of your calibrated range. You do not need to go beyond that range.
  • On-path rate is the share of scored movement time with the marker inside the path. Standing rests and tracking pauses are not scored. Bronze: at least 60%; silver: 75%; gold: 90%.
  • If tracking is lost, the path, timer and scoring pause. Get your whole body back in view, hold still and follow the recovery countdown.

Help with DogTrail

Cogni Drop: answer with side steps

Read the question and step toward the correct answer. The dog’s landing lane determines the answer. Squats and rotation do not select answers.

Choose a task and rule

  • Rock-paper-scissors: Win, Lose or Draw.
  • Math: Addition, Subtraction or Subtract 7.
  • Levels 1–3 change the falling speed.

Check the rule before you start. The first-time guide has two screens, and you can choose optional step practice. Start each question from a steady central stance.

Step, then bring your foot back

A side step moves the dog one lane. Bring that foot back to the center; the dog stays in the selected lane. Choose before it lands.

  • Correct answer: +1 point and a dog bark.
  • Wrong answer: −1 point. Landing in the center lane counts as unanswered: −1 point.
  • The result shows your score, correct, wrong and unanswered counts, and average answer time for correct answers.
  • Score bests and time bests each show a top three for the same settings. Time bests include only completed games with every answer correct.

Help with Cogni Drop and its records

Look back at your play

Open Play history from the title screen to see counts and play time by day, week, month or year. The three games are shown separately. Cogni Drop’s score and time bests appear on its end-of-game result screen.

Records are stored on your device. Setup, tracking pauses and recovery waits are not play time. An unfinished DogTrail course does not count as a completed course. Older records that were never saved cannot be reconstructed.

Switch between Japanese and English

Tap the underlined language control on the title screen and choose Japanese or English. Your choice is kept for the next launch. This changes the game and its help links.

The iOS camera permission message and home-screen app name follow the system’s language setting for the app. They may differ from your in-game selection. In English the home-screen name is WanMove; in Japanese it is わんムーブ.

Play safely

Do not start if you have pain, dizziness, strong fatigue or feel unwell. Stop immediately if you feel pain, dizziness or breathlessness during play. Avoid large movements or deep squats that feel uncomfortable.

If a child uses the app, an adult should check the device and surrounding space and supervise as needed.

Camera images and body-joint coordinates are processed on your device and are not saved or sent. Read the privacy policy.

WanMove is a general movement game. It is not designed or validated for diagnosis, treatment, rehabilitation or exercise prescription.
