Prepare for reliable tracking

Start with Level 1. DogDrop and Cogni Drop last one minute; DogTrail takes about one minute, plus setup.

  1. Clear space for a side step and a comfortable squat. Check objects, cables, steps, the floor and how you feel.
  2. Set the device securely at about waist height with the screen facing you.
  3. During the posture check or squat calibration, start about 1.5 m away and fit your whole body in view.
  4. Keep only the player in the camera view. Light the room and avoid bright light behind you.
  5. Face forward and stand still for a few seconds. Follow the framing and movement guidance.

Do not play if you have pain, dizziness, strong fatigue or feel unwell. Stop if you become unwell during play. Read the full guide.

Camera access

I declined camera access or want to change it

Likely cause: camera permission was declined or changed in Settings.

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Open Privacy & Security → Camera (Privacy → Camera on older iOS versions).
  3. Enable WanMove. It may be listed as わんムーブ or, for older versions, DogDrop.
  4. Close and reopen the app.

Expected: the camera preview appears during the posture check or squat calibration.

The preview is blank or setup does not advance

Possible causes: permission has not taken effect, the camera did not start, or your whole body is not visible.

  1. Check that camera access is enabled.
  2. Close other apps that use the camera, then restart WanMove.
  3. If needed, restart the device and check that its case or another object is not covering the front camera.
  4. Keep your head, knees and feet in view and follow the posture or squat instructions.

DogDrop and Cogni Drop advance after detecting your full body; DogTrail follows a stand–squat–stand sequence. The preview is shown only during these setup steps. Camera images and body-joint coordinates are not saved or sent.

My whole body is not detected

Setup is stuck or gameplay pauses automatically

Possible causes: your head, knees or feet are out of view; another person is visible; lighting is poor; or an object hides part of your body.

  1. Start about 1.5 m away and adjust until your head and feet fit in view.
  2. Keep other people out of the camera view. Make sure both knees and feet are unobstructed.
  3. Light the room and avoid facing away from a bright window or lamp.
  4. Face the camera and hold still for a few seconds. Cross your arms over your chest during DogTrail calibration.

Expected: setup advances, or the recovery guidance clears and practice or gameplay resumes.

DogDrop: steps and squats

A side step does not move the dog

Likely causes: feet are not visible, the step is too small to detect, or the previous step has not returned to center.

  1. Face forward with both knees and feet in view.
  2. Slide one foot sideways in the direction you want to move.
  3. Bring that foot back to the center and steady yourself before the next step.

Expected: each step moves the dog one lane. Bringing the foot back does not undo that move.

A squat does not make the dog drop faster

Face forward and keep your knees visible. Extend your arms comfortably in front of you, lower your hips slowly as if sitting on a chair, then stand up before trying again. Do not force a deeper squat.

Expected: one squat makes the dog drop faster; standing resets the input for the next squat.

The dog moves unexpectedly or repeats a move

Return both feet to the center and stand still facing forward. Make one movement at a time and return to a steady central stance after each. Put the device on a stable surface and keep other people or moving objects out of view.

Expected: a side step produces one lane change. A steady central stance produces no extra side-step or squat input; normal falling continues.

DogTrail: calibration and dog height

Squat calibration will not advance

Likely causes: your hips are hidden, you are still moving, or the device moved after calibration started.

  1. Face forward in good light, with your whole body and both hips visible.
  2. Cross your arms over your chest and stand naturally until the instruction advances.
  3. Slowly make a shallow squat and hold a comfortable height when asked.
  4. Slowly stand up and wait.
  5. If you moved the device or the range feels wrong, return to course selection and calibrate again.

Expected: all three steps advance and the app becomes ready after you stand. You do not need to match the example’s depth or force a deeper squat.

The dog’s height feels wrong or my on-path rate is low

Squatting moves the dog down; standing moves it up. Match the circular marker on the dog’s body—not its feet—to the path. Look ahead on the right and move slowly. Start with Level 1.

If standing does not bring the dog back up, return to course selection and repeat calibration. Use a comfortable range.

On-path rate measures scored movement time inside the path. Standing rests and tracking pauses are excluded. Bronze requires 60%, silver 75% and gold 90%.

How do the courses differ?

Each of Levels 1–3 has six patterns and each course has six down-and-up movements. Higher levels have narrower paths and more varied timing. One Level 3 movement reaches the bottom of your calibrated range; you do not need to squat below it.

If the movement feels uncomfortable, stop and choose a more comfortable range and level next time. DogTrail guide.

Cogni Drop: answers and results

I cannot reach the answer lane or choose the wrong side

Cogni Drop uses side steps only. Squats and rotation do not select answers.

  1. Face forward with your whole body visible and a steady central stance.
  2. Step one foot sideways toward the correct answer.
  3. When the dog moves one lane, bring your foot back to the center. The dog stays in that lane.
  4. Steady yourself before the next question. Use How to play from the setup screen and choose step practice if needed.

Expected: one side step moves the dog one lane. Your central stance does not move it sideways.

How are score and average answer time calculated?
  • Correct landing: +1 point.
  • Wrong landing: −1 point.
  • Landing in the center: unanswered, −1 point.

For correct answers, answer time runs from the question appearing to the final entry into the correct lane before landing. If you leave and return, the final entry is used. The average includes only correct answers. It includes movement time and is a game measure, not a medical reaction-time measurement.

After a game, view Results, Score bests and Time bests. Each best list has three entries for the same settings. Time bests require a completed game with every answer correct. Cogni Drop guide.

Pauses, sound and device support

The game stays paused

If the pause menu is open, tap Resume. If tracking guidance is shown, fit your whole body in view and hold a central stance for a few seconds. In DogTrail, wait for the recovery countdown; the path, timer and scoring remain stopped during the pause.

If it does not resume, return to the title and select the game and level or course again. Restart the app if the problem continues.

Expected: the guidance clears, the timer advances and movement controls resume.

I cannot hear sound

Turn up the device’s media volume and check whether audio is going to Bluetooth headphones or a speaker. Restart the app and check sound in another app if needed.

Each game has background music. Matching dogs in DogDrop and correct answers in Cogni Drop play a bark; wrong Cogni Drop answers play a low sound. There is a finish sound, but no button-click sound.

Does my device or OS work with WanMove?

Check Compatibility on the App Store page. Find your model and OS version in Settings → General → About. If an OS update is available, review it before deciding to update.

Expected: you can install or update the app and reach the front-camera setup.

Language and the app name

How do I switch languages?

Tap the underlined language control on the title screen and choose Japanese or English. The app remembers your choice. Help, support, privacy and credit links follow the selected game language.

Why is the camera message or home-screen name in a different language?

iOS controls the camera permission message and home-screen name using its language setting for the app. The in-game language selector does not change that setting. The name is WanMove in English and わんムーブ in Japanese. You can change the app’s preferred language in iOS Settings where available, then reopen it.

Changing language does not trigger the camera permission prompt again. If access was declined, follow camera access help.

Are English Samoyed messages generated by AI?

English uses prepared messages based on your local records. Japanese can use Apple’s on-device AI when available, with prepared messages as a fallback. The AI生成 label appears only for a generated Japanese closing.

Play records

A completed game is missing

Finish DogDrop or Cogni Drop when the timer ends, or reach the DogTrail goal, then check the result screen. Open Play history from the title and check the selected period and game.

Cogni Drop’s score and time bests are on its end-of-game result screen. Setup, tracking pauses and recovery waits do not count as play time. DogTrail includes standing rests during the course but excludes the starting wait and finish animation, so recorded time is shorter than the “about one minute” guidance.

Expected: a completed game updates the local totals. Periods that were never recorded cannot be reconstructed.

Records disappeared after reinstalling

History is stored only on your device, without an account or cloud sync. Deleting the app may delete that history. There is no in-app way to restore deleted records. New games are saved after reinstalling.

Safety and privacy

Leave clear space to either side and behind you. Keep the device stable. Move within a comfortable range for your ability, and stop if you feel pain, dizziness, breathlessness or become unwell. An adult should check setup and supervise children as needed.

Camera images and body-joint coordinates are processed on the device and are not saved or sent. Play history is local, without cloud sync. Optional analytics is sent only with consent. Read the privacy policy.

The Privacy settings screen is being prepared for a future update. It is not included in version 2.1.0, the current release checked on September 26, 2026. Current methods and planned controls are distinguished below.

How do I stop sharing usage data?

Current release: decline analytics on the initial screen. If you already agreed, stopping requires reinstalling, which may erase your local records. Read the current stopping method and precautions first.

Planned controls: title screen → Privacy settings → Do not share. This stops new analytics and discards unsent data. Deletion of data already received is a separate request.

Expected in the update: Sharing: off remains selected after a restart. Games and play history remain available.

How do I delete analytics already shared?

Current release: contact the privacy support address with the subject "WanMove data deletion request". Uninstalling the app does not delete data already received.

Planned controls: Privacy settings → Request analytics data deletion → Turn off and request deletion. Sharing turns off and deletion is requested for data linked to this installation. Data from other devices or before a reinstall may not be covered.

The notice that Unity has received the request confirms receipt, not completed server-side deletion. Contact privacy support about progress.

What if the update keeps showing “Deletion request saved”?

This describes the planned settings screen. The request stays on the device for retry.

  1. Check your connection and keep the app open.
  2. If an error and a Retry button are shown, choose Retry.
  3. Keep the app installed until Unity confirms receipt of your request.

Sharing stays off. You can choose Share again after Unity receives your request. If the status does not change while online, contact support with the message shown.

Will stopping sharing or requesting deletion erase my play history?

The planned settings controls keep your local play history, personal bests and language choice. Uninstalling the app itself may erase records; there is no cloud restore.
