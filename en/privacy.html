PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy

Updated September 23, 2026

This policy covers WanMove (わんムーブ, formerly DogDrop), including DogDrop, DogTrail and Cogni Drop.

English in-app support is in preparation. These English pages are available ahead of the update. English screen labels and language options describe the upcoming update.

Camera processing stays on your device

Camera images and body-joint coordinates are not saved or sent.

Play history is local

Scores and movement counts are stored on your device to provide the app’s features.

Usage data requires consent

Optional analytics is used for app improvement only if you agree.

1. Camera and body movements

WanMove uses the front camera on your iPhone or iPad to detect game controls. During the DogDrop and Cogni Drop posture checks and DogTrail squat calibration, the camera preview shows a framing guide and detected joint points or lines. The camera image is not displayed during practice or gameplay.

Camera images and body-joint coordinates are processed in real time on your device. They are not saved or sent outside the device.

DogTrail calibrates its control range from your standing position and a comfortable squat. The body coordinates used for calibration are not stored in play history or sent elsewhere.

2. Information stored on your device

Scores, play time, movement counts, personal bests and daily feedback are stored locally to provide game features. DogTrail also records on-path rates and medals. Cogni Drop handles scores, correct, wrong and unanswered counts, average answer times, and score and time bests for each set of settings locally. There is no account or cloud-sync feature.

Daily play counts and times for all three games are retained on your device without a rolling expiry, so you can review past months and years. Samoyed messages are created on the device from these summaries.

When the app is in Japanese and a supported device’s Apple on-device AI is available, it can generate a short closing message using categories such as the selected period, games played, and whether play-day counts increased or decreased from the previous week or month. Exact dates, counts, durations and complete histories are not passed to the model. This feature does not send records to an external AI service. English uses prepared messages; Japanese also uses prepared messages when AI is unavailable.

Japanese AI messages are generated automatically when available and carry an AI生成 label next to the heading. Generated text is held only temporarily while the app is running, not saved permanently. Inputs and generated text are not added to analytics.

If you consent to analytics, selected usage data described below is sent for analysis. Your stored play-history file itself is not sent.

3. Optional analytics

WanMove uses Unity Analytics only if you allow it on the initial consent screen.

Usage data that may be sent includes level, play time, lane count, rotation and cage settings, practice completion and step count, score, success and failure counts, best combo, accuracy, movement counts (total, right, left and squats), tracking-loss count and safety-pause state.

Information normally handled by Unity Analytics may include IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, an installation identifier, user ID and IDFV (identifier for vendor). The app does not request precise GPS location.

This information is used to understand feature usage and improve the app, not for advertising tracking.

Cogni Drop questions, answer outcomes, answer times and best records are handled locally. This feature does not add new analytics events.

4. Information the app does not save or send

The app does not save or send your name, contact details, camera images, body-joint coordinates, HealthKit health records or medical records.

5. Withdrawing consent and deleting data

You can decline analytics on the initial screen by choosing Do not share. If you previously agreed and want to stop future sharing, delete and reinstall the app, then choose Do not share on the initial screen.

To request deletion of analytics already sent to Unity Analytics, email the address below with the subject “WanMove data deletion request”. Requests using the names わんムーブ or DogDrop are also accepted. We may ask for the app version and device information as needed to handle the request.

Deleting the app may also remove your local play history. The app cannot restore deleted history from the cloud.

dogdrop.support@gmail.com

6. External services

Optional analytics uses Unity Analytics. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy for Unity’s privacy information.

7. Changes to this policy

This page will be updated if the app’s features or handling of information changes.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact details on our support page.
